# Domain: Business Environment

Original practice questions. Try each one before opening the answer.

---

**1.** Halfway through the project, a new regulation changes a requirement of the deliverable. What should the project manager do FIRST?

- A. Ignore it until the project ends
- B. Assess the impact on the project and raise a change request through change control
- C. Stop the project
- D. Ask the team to redo all the work

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B.** Compliance is mandatory. Assess the impact first, then change the scope through the proper channel and inform the sponsor.
</details>

---

**2.** The project closed on time and on budget, but six months later the expected savings are not visible. Who is usually responsible for realising the benefits after closure?

- A. The project manager
- B. The business owner or operations
- C. The vendor
- D. The project team

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B.** The project delivers the capability. The business owner is responsible for using it and realising the benefits.
</details>

---

**3.** Which document justifies why the organisation should invest in the project?

- A. Project charter
- B. Business case
- C. Risk register
- D. Communications plan

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B.** The business case sets out the need, options, costs and expected benefits that justify the investment. The charter then authorises the project.
</details>

---

**4.** Users resist a new system the project is about to deliver. What is the BEST action?

- A. Order them to use it
- B. Engage them early, explain the reasons, and support them with training and communication
- C. Delay delivery until all users agree
- D. Remove the resisting users from the user group

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B.** Resistance is reduced by early involvement, a clear reason for the change and support during adoption. Forcing (A) or removing (D) people raises resistance.
</details>

---

**5.** Management has to cut one of three projects. How should it decide?

- A. By project size
- B. By the manager's seniority
- C. By alignment with strategic objectives and expected value
- D. By the project that started first

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C.** Portfolio decisions rest on strategic alignment and value, not on size, seniority or order of start.
</details>

---

**6.** To save time, the team proposes skipping a mandatory regulatory test. What should the project manager do?

- A. Agree, because the schedule is critical
- B. Skip it only for low-risk items
- C. Refuse to skip it and look for another way to recover time
- D. Skip it and note the risk in the register

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C.** Legal and regulatory requirements are not traded for schedule or cost. Recover time with other options such as fast tracking other work, if approved.
</details>

---

**7.** A competitor launches a similar product during your project. What should the project manager do?

- A. Continue as planned without change
- B. Update the risk and opportunity analysis and discuss the effect on the business case with the sponsor
- C. Cancel the project
- D. Speed up by cutting quality

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B.** An external change may affect the business case. Analyse and bring it to the sponsor, who decides whether to adjust scope or direction.
</details>

---

**8.** The project manager finds that the project no longer fits the organisation's strategy. What should be done?

- A. Continue because the plan was approved
- B. Report it to the sponsor or governance body so they can decide whether to continue, change or stop
- C. Change the scope without telling anyone
- D. Cancel the project

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B.** The project manager reports the finding and recommends. Continuing, changing or stopping the project is a governance decision.
</details>

---

**9.** The sponsor wants value delivered early and the requirements are uncertain. Which approach fits BEST?

- A. A detailed predictive plan for the whole scope
- B. Incremental delivery of a minimum viable product with feedback
- C. Waiting until all requirements are known
- D. Outsourcing the whole project

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B.** Incremental delivery gives value early and lets feedback shape the later scope when requirements are uncertain.
</details>

---

**10.** Which type of PMO directly manages the projects and is responsible for their results?

- A. Supportive
- B. Controlling
- C. Directive
- D. Advisory

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C.** A directive PMO manages projects directly. A supportive PMO provides templates and advice, and a controlling PMO checks compliance with standards.
</details>
