## Concepts you must be able to explain without notes

- Who is accountable for benefits after the project closes
- What a project manager does when a project no longer fits the strategy
- Why a compliance requirement is never traded away for time or cost
- How to respond when a regulation or the market changes mid-project
- Why a project that is delivered but not adopted has not delivered value
