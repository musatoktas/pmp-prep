# The 8-week plan

| Week | Focus | Target by Sunday |
|---|---|---|
| [Week 1](week-1.md) | The exam, lifecycles, the PM role and the mindset | 150 questions done, course started |
| [Week 2](week-2.md) | People I: leadership, conflict, stakeholders, communication | 170 questions done, 70%+ on People |
| [Week 3](week-3.md) | People II: agile mindset, team performance, virtual teams | 170 questions done, 70%+ on People |
| [Week 4](week-4.md) | Process I: scope, schedule, cost, earned value | 170 questions done, 70%+ on Process |
| [Week 5](week-5.md) | Process II: quality, resources, risk, procurement | 170 questions done, 70%+ on Process |
| [Week 6](week-6.md) | Process III: integration, change, closing, hybrid delivery | 170 questions done, 70%+ on Process |
| [Week 7](week-7.md) | Business Environment: compliance, value, strategy, change | 170 questions done, 70%+ on Business Environment |
| [Week 8](week-8.md) | Two full mock exams, weak areas, exam | 75%+ on both mocks |

Roughly 1,200 practice questions in total. The 35 hours of education should be finished by the end of week 4 so that you can send the PMI application and book the exam.

## Daily shape (Monday to Friday)

| Block | Time | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| Read or watch | 40 min | The day's topic |
| Condense | 20 min | One-page summary, 5 flashcards |
| Practise | 45 min | 25 to 30 timed questions |
| Review | 15 min | Every explanation, miss logged in [`tracker.md`](../tracker.md) |

Saturday: 4 hours, 60 mixed questions and a full review. Sunday: rest or 30 minutes with the wrong-answer log.
