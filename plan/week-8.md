# Week 8: Mock exams, weak areas and the exam

Goal: show you can sit 180 questions in 240 minutes at 75% or better, and fix what is still weak.

| Day | Topic | Practise |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Review your wrong-answer log, group misses by domain and task | 40 Qs on the two weakest areas |
| Tue | Full mock exam 1 (180 Qs, 240 min, one sitting) | 180 Qs |
| Wed | Review mock 1: every miss, every guess | Read, write flashcards |
| Thu | Full mock exam 2 (180 Qs, 240 min, one sitting) | 180 Qs |
| Fri | Review mock 2, formula sheet, last look at weak topics | 40 Qs on weak topics |
| Sat | Light review only. Check the booking and the ID | None, or 20 easy Qs |
| Sun | Rest | |

The exam is best booked for the Monday or Tuesday after week 8, when the material is fresh.

## Final checklist

- [ ] 75%+ on both mocks on a simulator that matches the July 2026 exam
- [ ] Formula sheet written from memory and checked (EVM, three-point estimate, communication channels)
- [ ] Wrong-answer log reviewed twice
- [ ] Exam-day logistics done, see [`exam-day.md`](../exam-day.md)
