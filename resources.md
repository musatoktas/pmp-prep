# Resources

Check every fact against the current PMI documents. The exam changed on 9 July 2026, so confirm that any third-party material covers the new content.

## Official

| Resource | Use |
|---|---|
| PMP Exam Content Outline (PMI website) | The source of truth for domains, tasks and weights. Read it in week 1 and again in week 8 |
| PMI Certification Handbook | Eligibility, application, audit, exam rules and retake policy |
| PMBOK Guide and Agile Practice Guide | Reference reading. PMI members get digital copies, so check what your membership includes |
| Ethics and Professional Conduct (PMI Code of Ethics) | Short and worth reading once. Ethics questions follow it |

## Education (35 contact hours)

Choose a course that states it covers the current exam content and that PMI accepts for the 35-hour requirement. A PMI Authorized Training Partner course is the safest choice. Finish it by the end of week 4.

## Practice

| Resource | Use |
|---|---|
| A paid simulator that matches the July 2026 exam | Main question source. Do the mock exams in week 8 here |
| [`questions/`](questions/) in this repository | Warm-up only |
| The Scrum Guide (free, scrumguides.org) | Roles, events and artefacts. About 15 pages |

## How to use a question bank

1. Do questions in timed sets of 25 to 30.
2. Read every explanation, including for questions you got right.
3. Log every miss in [`tracker.md`](tracker.md) with the reason: knowledge gap, misread qualifier, or second-guessing.
4. Never use exam dumps or leaked questions. They break PMI's rules and teach memorising, not judgement.
