## Concepts you must be able to explain without notes

- Who may approve a change, and the steps from request to implementation
- The difference between a baseline, a forecast and an actual
- How agile change differs from predictive change
- What must happen when a project is stopped halfway
- How lessons learned are captured during the project, not only at the end
