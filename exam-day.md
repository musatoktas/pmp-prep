# Exam day

Check the current PMI Certification Handbook for every rule below. This page is a checklist, not the authority.

## Before you book

1. Confirm eligibility: 36 months leading projects within the last 8 years with a four-year degree (60 months without), and 35 contact hours of education or a CAPM certificate.
2. Write each project in the application with real dates, your real role and a short description. PMI can audit the application, so keep contact details for each project's manager or sponsor.
3. Decide on PMI membership by comparing the total cost of member and non-member exam fees on PMI's fee page.
4. Book the exam for the Monday or Tuesday after week 8.

## Test centre or online

| | Test centre | Online proctored |
|---|---|---|
| Good for | No home-network or room risk | No travel |
| Check | Route, time, ID | System test, camera, clear desk and room, stable connection |

## During the exam

- 180 questions in 240 minutes is about 80 seconds a question. Check the break rules in the handbook and use the breaks.
- Mark long calculation questions and come back to them.
- Read the qualifier: first, best, most likely, next.
- Choose the answer a project manager would choose: analyse, talk to people, use change control, protect value and compliance.
- Do not change an answer without a clear reason.

## Not allowed

Using AI tools, notes, phones or any other help during the exam, and sharing or using exam questions, are violations of PMI's rules and Code of Ethics. The consequences can include a cancelled result and a ban. Use AI to study before the exam, never during it.
