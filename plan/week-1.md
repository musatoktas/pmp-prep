## Concepts you must be able to explain without notes

- The three domains and what a typical question in each looks like
- When a predictive, agile or hybrid approach fits, and why tailoring matters
- Strong, weak and balanced matrix structures, and the project manager's authority in each
- Directive, supportive and controlling PMOs
- What the sponsor decides and what the project manager decides
